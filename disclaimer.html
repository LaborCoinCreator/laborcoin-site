Disclaimer and Risk Notice

Experimental protocol

LaborCoin is experimental public infrastructure. Revision 7.1 is a source candidate and has not been compiled, audited, deployed, or activated.

No financial promise

LABR has no guaranteed value, liquidity, fiat redemption, appreciation, dividend amount, or market. Exchange V6 is denominated in POL, whose external value can change substantially.

Equal dividends are conditional

A verified wallet must hold at least 1 LABR to accrue one equal share. Amounts depend on sell activity, eligible-holder count, contract operation, and available POL. No dividend amount or frequency is promised.

Identity limitations

Human Passport scoring reduces ordinary multi-wallet abuse but does not prove one natural person controls only one wallet. Verification is permanent and cannot be revoked, transferred, or recovered.

Immutable-contract risk

The final design has no upgrade or emergency recovery path. A defect, mistaken constant, compromised verifier authorization, or unexpected interaction may be permanent.

Governance and recipient risk

Members may approve an incorrect, ineffective, controversial, or malicious recipient. A completed Aragon DAO transfer is irreversible. LaborCoin cannot guarantee real-world worker benefit.

Infrastructure and legal risk

Polygon, Human Passport, the verifier, RPCs, hosting, DNS, browser dependencies, and Aragon permissions can fail or change. Legal, tax, labor, token, and DAO treatment varies by jurisdiction and may change.

No advice

Nothing on this site is legal, investment, tax, financial, employment, or organizational advice. Review the source, whitepaper, test status, and independent assessments before participating.
