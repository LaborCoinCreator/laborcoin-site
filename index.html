What This Is

LaborCoin is a system for building a shared treasury that can support workers during strikes and collective action.

The strategy is simple: contribute regularly, grow a common fund, and deploy it when workers need support.

Revision 7.1 equal-holder candidate

The Revision 7.1 contracts are a precompilation source candidate and have not been deployed. Interactive pages remain disabled until all seven contracts are compiled, tested, deployed, and verified against their final runtime commitments.

The existing Aragon DAO continues to hold treasury POL. The prior V4/V7/V13 deployment remains a historical record until the coordinated Revision 7.1 cutover is complete.

How the system works

  1. Verify a direct Polygon wallet once through the immutable Identity Registry using a Human Passport score of at least 15.
  2. Acquire or sell LABR through identity-gated Exchange V6 using an exact LABR amount and a deterministic POL quote.
  3. Receive one equal dividend share while verified and holding at least 1 LABR, regardless of the number of LABR held.
  4. Optionally register for governance and receive exactly one nontransferable LABRV membership token.
  5. Create and vote on fixed Treasury Transfer proposals.
  6. Execute successful proposals as one native-POL transfer through the existing Aragon DAO.

Final candidate components

Identity Registry V1: permanent score-15 EIP-712 verification shared by the Exchange, dividend system, and governance registration.

LABR V3: fixed supply, strict wallet and transaction limits, official-exchange cooldown, and one equal dividend unit per verified holder with at least 1 LABR.

Exchange V6: identity-gated integral POL-denominated bonding curve, reserve accounting, treasury contributions, and the permanent official-exchange whale bar.

Registration V6.1: one-time governance registration using the shared immutable identity status and a minimum balance of 1 LABR.

LaborVote V9.1: one nontransferable membership unit per registered participant.

Proposal Text Policy V1.0.1: immutable on-chain description screening.

Governance V15.1: open voting for every member registered before the deadline, a final deadline electorate, fixed thresholds, and direct Aragon DAO execution.

Permanent economic rules

  • 1,000,000,000 LABR fixed supply.
  • 10,000 LABR maximum wallet.
  • 5,000 LABR maximum transaction.
  • 12-hour trade cooldown.
  • Official buying and selling require permanent score-15 identity verification.
  • Wallets holding more than 10,000 LABR cannot buy or sell through the official exchange.
  • 10% of purchase input goes to the DAO.
  • Sales allocate 5% to the DAO and 5% equally among verified holders with at least 1 LABR.
  • Holding more LABR does not increase a participant's dividend share.

Governance rules

  • Activation at 50 registered members.
  • 14-day voting period and 7-day execution window.
  • 25% participation and 67% approval, both rounded upward.
  • Maximum transfer of 5% of the DAO's current POL balance.
  • One active proposal per member and one vote per eligible member.
  • Members who register before an active proposal deadline may vote on that proposal.
  • The final participation denominator is fixed at the voting deadline.
  • No tokenomics changes, upgrades, arbitrary calls, or administrative overrides.
