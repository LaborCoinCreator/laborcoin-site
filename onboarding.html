LaborCoin Onboarding

Permanent identity verification enables official economic participation. Governance registration is a separate optional step.

1. Set up and connect a direct Polygon wallet

LaborCoin works with MetaMask and other compatible self-custody wallet services. You do not need MetaMask if you already have another compatible direct wallet.

The wallet must be an externally owned account. Smart-contract wallets, multisignature wallets, account-abstraction wallets, transaction relays, and indirect calls are rejected by the candidate contracts.

Get MetaMask

2. Acquire POL

POL pays Polygon transaction fees and is the direct denomination of Exchange V6. LaborCoin makes no fiat-price guarantee.

3. Build a Human Passport score

Open Human Passport and build a score of at least 15.000. The score gate reduces simple multi-wallet abuse while remaining accessible to newer wallets.

Open Human Passport

4. Verify your wallet once

On the Exchange or DAO page, request a one-hour EIP-712 authorization and submit it to Identity Registry V1. Verification is permanent and reused for official buying, selling, equal-holder dividends, claims, and optional governance registration.

The authorization is bound to your wallet, score, fixed scorer ID hash, nonce, Polygon chain ID, exact Registry address, and expiry.

5. Buy an exact LABR amount

Enter up to 5,000 LABR. Review the curve reserve contribution, 10% DAO contribution, and total POL input. Your resulting balance cannot exceed 10,000 LABR.

A wallet holding more than 10,000 LABR is barred from both buying and selling through the official exchange.

Open Exchange

6. Receive one equal dividend share

A verified wallet holding at least 1 LABR receives one equal share of holder-dividend deposits. Holding more LABR does not increase dividend weight.

Available POL dividends are displayed and claimed on the Exchange page.

7. Optional governance registration

While verified and holding at least 1 LABR, sign the worker-centered attestation and register once through Registration V6. Registration mints exactly one nontransferable LABRV.

Registration

8. Participate in governance

After 50 members exist, registered members may create one active Treasury Transfer proposal and vote once per proposal. Members joining after a proposal snapshot cannot vote on that proposal.

Governance

9. Sell through the official Exchange

A verified wallet at or below 10,000 LABR may sell up to 5,000 LABR after the 12-hour cooldown. Selling below 1 LABR stops future dividend accrual but does not erase already accrued claims.
